## Getting your first funded account — example requirements

![[Pasted image 20260717062813.png]]

- **$3,000 profit target. Don't lose more than $2,000.**
- **EOD trail** = end-of-day drawdown, measured at close.
- **No more than 50% of profits in a day** (consistency rule).
- No matter how much you make, **you can't lose $2K in a row** — think of
  it as a rubber band attached to your profit.

## What it's NOT about — and what kills 90%

![[Pasted image 20260717063009.png]]

**It's not about discipline. It's not about screen time. It's not about
talent.** — "If those worked, I'd have been profitable in year one. I
wasn't." The answer: a system.

![[Pasted image 20260717063025.png]]

**The three killers:**
1. **Overtrading** — no clear criteria; 5 hours staring at charts gets
   you nowhere. *(My rule: killzone only + close the screen.)*
2. **Revenge trading** — trades feel personal, logic dies, the account
   dies with it. *(My rule: max 2 stops/day, screen closes at -2/-3%.)*
3. **Strategy hopping** — like a diet you change every Monday; nothing
   compounds. *(My rule: one system — Dreyko/MMXM — no change until the
   journal data proves otherwise.)*
